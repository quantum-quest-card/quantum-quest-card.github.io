Quantum Quest
日本語 by Requid Osaka
Serious Game · Quantum Futures · RRI

Explore the near future of quantum technology through strategic play.

Quantum Quest is a card game designed to help participants explore the ethical, legal, and social implications of quantum technologies, as well as questions of responsible research and innovation.

Through dice rolls, resource tokens, negotiation, mission cards, and unexpected events, players experience the dynamics of technological competition, cooperation, strategic decision-making, and social uncertainty.

Quantum Quest is designed as an experiential learning tool for discussing ELSI — ethical, legal, and social issues — and RRI — responsible research and innovation — in the context of quantum technology.

What You Can Learn

Systems Thinking

Understand how scientific progress, policy, markets, and society interact in quantum innovation.

RRI Basics

Get introduced to core RRI ideas: anticipation, inclusion, reflexivity, and responsiveness.

Strategic Collaboration

Experience trade-offs between competition and cooperation while navigating uncertain futures.

Who Is It For?

Quantum Quest is designed for students, researchers, educators, and anyone interested in the social dimensions of emerging technologies. It can be used in workshops, classrooms, and public engagement events.
